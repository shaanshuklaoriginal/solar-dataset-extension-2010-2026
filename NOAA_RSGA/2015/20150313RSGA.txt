southward component of Bz reached -7 nT at 13/1125Z. Electrons greater
than 2 MeV at geosynchronous orbit reached a peak level of 144 pfu.

to be at unsettled to minor storm levels on day one (14 Mar), unsettled
to active levels on day two (15 Mar) and quiet to unsettled levels on
day three (16 Mar). Protons have a slight chance of crossing threshold
on days one, two, and three (14 Mar, 15 Mar, 16 Mar).
