IIA.  Geophysical Activity Summary 09/2100Z to 10/2100Z:
The geomagnetic field has been quiet.  The ACE spacecraft observed a
continued decline in the solar wind speed, averaging 375 km/s.  The
greater than 2 MeV electron flux at geosynchronous orbit reached
high levels today.

expected to be quiet for day one (11 April).  Quiet to unsettled
conditions, with isolated active periods, are expected on day two
and three (12-13 April).  This activity is expected due to the 08
April full halo CME.
