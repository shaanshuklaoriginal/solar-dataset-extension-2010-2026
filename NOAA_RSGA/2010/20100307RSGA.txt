SDF Number 066 Issued at 2200Z on 07 Mar 2010
IA.  Analysis of Solar Active Regions and Activity from  06/2100Z
to 07/2100Z:  Solar activity was very low. Only low-level B-class
flares were observed during the period.
IB.  Solar Activity Forecast:  Solar activity is expected to be very
low for the next three days (08 - 10 March).
IIA.  Geophysical Activity Summary 06/2100Z to 07/2100Z:
The geomagnetic field was mostly quiet during the past 24 hours. A
single period of unsettled conditions was observed at mid-latitudes
between 07/0600Z and 07/0900Z.
IIB.  Geophysical Activity Forecast:  The geomagnetic field is
expected to be at quiet to unsettled levels on days one and two (08
- 09 March) in response to the CME observed on 06 March. Geomagnetic
field activity is expected to decrease to quiet levels on day three
(10 March) as the CME effects subside.
