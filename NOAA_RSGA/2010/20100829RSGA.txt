IA.  Analysis of Solar Active Regions and Activity from  28/2100Z
to 29/2100Z:  Solar activity has been at very low levels for the past

sunspot group with a beta magnetic classification.

IIA.  Geophysical Activity Summary 28/2100Z to 29/2100Z:
The geomagnetic field has been at quiet levels for the past 24 hours.
ACE solar wind readings indicated velocities gradually decreasing 
from 580 to 450 km/s during the period. This decrease in velocity
was associated with the waning effects of the latest coronal hole
high speed stream. The greater than 2 MeV electron flux at
geosynchronous orbit was at high levels throughout the period.

expected to be at quiet levels for the next three days (30 August -
01 September).
