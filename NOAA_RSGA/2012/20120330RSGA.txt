IA.  Analysis of Solar Active Regions and Activity from  29/2100Z
to 30/2100Z:  Solar activity has been very low for the past 24 hours.
Areas of plage and a few small spots have rotated onto the visible
disk near the area of old Region 1429, but this region isnt as
impressive as anticipated.  There were two eruptive filaments from
the northern hemisphere, one at 29/1800Z and another at 30/1436Z
that were associated with CMEs, neither of which is expected to be
geoeffective.

with a slight chance for an isolated M-class flare.

expected to remain at quiet levels for the next three days.
